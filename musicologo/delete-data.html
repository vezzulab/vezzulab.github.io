Delete your data

Musicologo · El Combate, by vezzulab · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of Musicologo · El Combate (developer: vezzulab). The app has no accounts. Your data lives on your own tablet, and only a small part of it can reach our online service.

1. Data on your tablet

Settings, sets, sampler sounds, your DJ name and your booking details are stored only on your tablet. To delete them:

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Android Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Musicologo ▸ Storage ▸ Clear data.

2. Data in our online service (only if you used requests by QR over the internet)

These are deleted automatically:

  • The address of your tablet inside the network: after 15 minutes.
  • Your DJ name, booking details and the song playing: after 4 hours.
  • Guests' request counters: after 4 hours.
  • The song list of the party: after up to 7 days.

One item is kept until you ask to delete it: an anonymous identifier of your tablet (a random code and an encrypted secret). It contains no name, email or phone number.

3. How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vezzulab@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data - Musicologo.
  2. Include the address printed on your poster (it looks like .../d/xxxxxxxx). The last 8 characters identify your tablet. You can also find it in the app: menu ▸ Requests by QR.
  3. We delete the identifier and anything else linked to it as soon as possible, and within 30 days. We will confirm by email.

En español: la app no tiene cuentas. Tus datos están en tu tableta; para borrarlos desinstala la app o usa «Borrar datos» en Ajustes de Android. Lo que llega a nuestro servicio en línea se borra solo (15 minutos a 7 días). Para pedir que borremos el identificador anónimo de tu tableta, escribe a vezzulab@gmail.com con el asunto «Borrar mis datos - Musicologo» y la dirección de tu cartel. Lo borramos lo antes posible y como máximo en 30 días.
