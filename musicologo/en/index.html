YOU BRING THE MUSIC, SHE BRINGS THE FIGHT

Step into the ring.
Sound like a club.

Any genre, any party. You bring the folders and decide how much control you want: let the app mix for you, or take the decks yourself.

Round 1

Self-mixing

You pick the folders and the app does the rest: it matches the rhythm of the songs and switches on the beat. If the tempos are not alike, it does a clean fade, with no harsh cuts.

  • The bass of one song leaves while the other's comes in.
  • Play your music from the tablet, from a server or from your NAS.
  • When the list runs out, it keeps going with new songs.
Round 2

You are in control

Tap MANUAL and the decks are yours. The disc scratches. The outer ring is the whole song: tap the minute you want and it stays there.

  • CUE, 8 hot cues per deck and loops.
  • Per-channel and 10-band equalizer.
  • Sets by folder: you choose how many songs from each.
Round 3 · Try it

Unlimited sampler

Keep all the sounds you want. The console shows 19 buttons and, at the end, the + button that opens your gallery to add or remove. Tap the buttons and open the gallery.

TAP A BUTTON
19 / 19 IN THE SAMPLER
Sound gallery

Everything you want fits here. ADD puts a sound in the sampler and REMOVE takes it out (it stays saved). Maximum 19 at a time on the console.

These are the same sounds as in the app. In the app you add your own.

Round 4

Connect your controller

Plug your DJ controller into the tablet by USB, with an OTG adapter, and the app recognizes it by itself. You teach it each control and it is saved.

1Plug it in by USB. The MIDI icon turns green.
2Tap LEARN on a function.
3Move the knob or the button. Done, assigned.
  • More than 70 functions: play, CUE, loops, hot cues, jog and scratch, EQ, filters, faders, crossfader, volume and 12 sampler buttons.
  • Works with standard USB MIDI controllers, of any brand.

We are testing with real controllers: tell us which one you use and we will put it first.

Round 5

The floor requests

A poster with a QR code. The guest scans it with their phone, without installing anything or joining your WiFi, and requests their song.

  • Search from A to Z, only within what is playing at your party.
  • Few requests per person, so they do not take over your party.
  • It tells them “3 songs before yours”.

And it promotes you, too

The same page the guest opens shows your booking details: phone, WhatsApp, email, website, Instagram and Facebook, each with its own button to call or write with one tap.

  • Whoever is having fun at your party sees how to hire you right when they feel like it most.
  • You type your details once in Settings and they show up on every request page.
Round 6

Light up
the floor

The lights pulse with the beat and change color on every bar. You turn them on from the deck, and when the party ends they go back to how they were.
